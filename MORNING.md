# One morning prototype

The normal Start button begins a twelve-minute morning before the Hagia Sophia
service. Bells sound at 4, 8, 10 and 12 minutes; after 15 minutes the service has
ended, but the city remains playable. C reveals precise time; bells are the default.

The player starts carrying the existing bread parcel for Theodora, the water
carrier. Quiet delivery, public praise, or an unfinished promise are recalled
at church. The entrance marker is in the church forecourt on the main road,
not a new navigable church interior. E deliberately enters; battles are optional.
Arrival before twelve minutes, between twelve and fifteen, or later produces
an on-time, late, or missed-service account. There is no numerical holiness score.

Racing earns 20 gold, completed harbour work earns 10, and first landmark
resolutions earn 6. Proud market choices earn 5. Each source pays once per morning;
practice cannot mint money. A market stall sells swift shoes for 20 gold, increasing
walking and running speed by 15%. These rewards are optional distractions.

J opens the historical journal from the street and pauses time. Market dialogue,
battle introductions/menus, rest screens, and hidden tabs also pause the clock.
Exploration, world activities, and active combat consume time. The existing site
notes supply the journal rather than introducing another content backend.

Elapsed time, purchases and earnings live in the existing save flags. New Morning
uses the existing restart flow and clears the run. This first prototype has one
fixed responsibility and one purchasable upgrade; rotating responsibilities,
patron invitation chains, additional skills, and a persistent discovery journal
remain future work. Legacy Ladder endings remain accessible in debug tests that
do not start a morning.
