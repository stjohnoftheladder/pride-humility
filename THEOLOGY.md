# THEOLOGY.md — Pride & Humility: content & sources

This prototype is a playable catechism. Every mechanic is a teaching made
touchable; every teaching is drawn from the Orthodox ascetic tradition. This
document maps them so you (and Corey, and any reviewer) can check the theology.

## 1. The narrative spine

**Premise (Egri):** *Pride exalts only to destroy; humility, born of
repentance, restores communion.*

**Character spine (2–6 words):**
- The pilgrim *wants*: "to be seen as great" (the prideful pull)
- The pilgrim *needs*: "to be known by God" (the humble way)

**The journey:** a pilgrim enters a monastery gate, passes three thresholds
(greed, anger, pride), and reaches the Ladder gate. Each threshold asks the
same question: *by whose strength do you climb?* The answer is not spoken —
it is enacted in FIGHT, PRAY, ALMS, MERCY.

## 2. The three thresholds

| Encounter | Passion | Ladder of Divine Ascent | The choice |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Tempter | Greed (φιλαργυρία) | rung 17 (avarice) | take the gold / refuse in gratitude |
| The Wounded Brother | Anger (ὀργή) | rung 8 (anger), rung 9 (malice) | strike back / forgive |
| The Demon of Pride | Pride (ὑπερηφανία) | rung 23 (pride), rung 22 (vainglory) | fight to win / endure to be spared |

St. John Climacus: pride is the *last* of the passions — it rises as you
climb, and attacks hardest near the summit. Hence the boss scales with your
pride meter, and its mercy is gated on grace: **the proud man cannot spare,
because sparing requires humility.**

## 3. Mechanic → source mapping

| Mechanic | Teaching | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **FIGHT** damages the enemy, briefly restores HP, and shortens the next assault, but raises PRIDE | Violence tempts through immediate felt control; that relief feeds the passion and strengthens the last adversary | Climacus, Ladder 23; Cassian, *Institutes* XII.2 (pride is the root of all evil, the demons' first sin) |
| **PRAY** action (+grace, with an immediate stamina cost) and hold-`Space` shield | The Jesus Prayer — prayer of the heart as the Christian's true defense; stillness is chosen without becoming an effortless power-up | The Philokalia; the Jesus Prayer: *"Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me, a sinner"* (the Publican's prayer, Luke 18:13) |
| Shield runs on stamina ("PRAYER" bar) | Prayer is effort, not magic — it must be sustained; grace is a gift, never earned, but effort disposes the heart | Climacus, Ladder 28 (prayer); the teaching on unceasing prayer (1 Thess 5:17) |
| **ALMS** — share bread & water | Almsgiving loosens the grip of possessions; small, concrete mercy | Ladder 17; Matthew 25:35–40 |
| **MERCY / Spare** | Forbearance and the sparing of life; "mercy rejoices against judgment" | James 2:13; the Publican & the Pharisee (Luke 18:9–14) |
| The heart is the soul; HP loss = soul-darkening | Damage is not just pain — it is the soul growing cold; prayer slows the assault | The ascetic teaching that the passions "wound the heart" (cf. Mark 7:21–23) |
| **Pride reddens and dims the world**; the boss grows with your pride | The proud man's world shrinks to himself; his own progress feeds his adversary | Ladder 23.1–5; Cassian XII.7 |
| **Confession at the altar** restores HP and provisions; its grace increase is received once | Repentance is medicine, not a score loop; sacramental restoration cannot be farmed as currency | Ladder 5 (repentance); the order of confession |
| **Falling in battle** → "Repent & rise again" | Even falls are given to the humble as lessons; the door of repentance is never locked | Climacus on the fall and rising; Proverbs 24:16 |
| **Ending: THE LADDER IS NOT CLIMBED** | Theosis is not achievement; it is reception — kenosis, the emptying of Christ | Philippians 2:5–11; James 4:6; 1 Peter 5:5 |
| **Ending: THE EMPTY SUMMIT** | Pride's end is loneliness — the fall of the day-star | Isaiah 14:12; Ladder 23 |
| **Ending: A PARTLY-WASHED PILGRIM** | A mixed life: the way is open but must be walked again, lighter | 1 Corinthians 15:34 |

## 4. Design intent (for Corey)

- **Palette:** the byzantine gold-on-dark tokens are already the liturgical
  mood — candle warmth vs. cold red as pride rises.
- **The heart as protagonist:** the soul is a tiny red heart in a gold box —
  Undertale's convention made Orthodox: the heart is *the* subject of
  asceticism ("keep thy heart with all diligence", Prov 4:23).
- **The prayer shield** should read as a halo-glow, not a force-field — gold
  light, not blue tech.
- **Mercy** should feel like *releasing* — the enemy shrinks, a beam falls,
  the palette warms. FIGHT should feel *heavy* — the world dims a degree.
- **Rooms preach before dialogue:** unused gold fills Greed's chamber, a stained
  pallet embodies the Brother's grievance, and Pride's walls grow watching eyes.

## 5. Sources consulted

- St. John Climacus, *The Ladder of Divine Ascent* (rungs 5, 8, 9, 17, 22, 23, 25, 28)
- St. John Cassian, *The Institutes* (Book XII, "On the Spirit of Pride")
- The Holy Gospels: Luke 18:9–14; James 4:6; 1 Peter 5:5; Philippians 2:5–11; Isaiah 14:12
- The Jesus Prayer (Philokalic tradition)
- St. Isaac of Nineveh on humility and mercy
