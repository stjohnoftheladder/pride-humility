YOUR MORNING

  1. Carry the bread to Theodora, the water carrier. The line at the bottom of the screen gives her direction and distance.
  2. Reach Hagia Sophia before the service bell. Four bells ring through the morning; the clock rests while you read or talk.
  3. Signs marked APPROACH · E along the road offer something to do. You cannot do them all before the bell, so choose. What you do, and how, is remembered.
